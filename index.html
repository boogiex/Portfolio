Background

About

I'm a data-driven, commercially-minded martech professional. My career started with an engineering degree, which gave me a technical foundation I carried straight into In Digital, where that technical acumen grew fast, helping clients set up paywall platforms and understand the best ways to measure success and optimise their product stack.

That grounding shaped how I approached experimentation: it wasn't just about running tests, it was about validating what actually moved the needle for users and, in turn, grew our clients' customer bases. That meant getting comfortable with complex analysis, including LTV modelling, ad revenue balance, business forecasting and deep consumer insight, and using it to understand the full user lifecycle: everything from before a user lands on-site, through their on-site journey, to conversion and retention.

That breadth of experience opened the door to a secondment inside the Financial Times, where I now lead the AI Paywall workstream, setting strategy for how the AI paywall model drives commercial growth, which means going deep on both strategy and the mechanics of reinforcement learning.

Today my work spans the full user lifecycle: selecting and vetting products and vendors to build the right tech and data stack, setting the strategy for how platforms are used, and making sure every platform in that stack earns its keep, tracking ROI and running the experimentation programmes that prove commercial impact. What ties it together is a long-standing interest in how numbers and data explain user behaviour, and how that understanding drives commercial growth. That gives me a rounded skill set across analytics, technical implementation, strategy and user growth.

Case studies

Work

A handful of projects spanning platform selection, data & measurement, and AI. Client specifics are generalised where confidentiality applies.

Financial Times · Secondment

Leading strategy for the FT's AI-powered paywall

→

The FT's AI-powered paywall needed to make smarter access decisions across the whole onsite journey — which meant someone owning the strategy behind how the model actually used commercial signals like LTV to drive performance.

Approach

Led strategy for the paywall's onsite journey, working closely with the model to understand how customer lifetime value influenced its decisioning and overall performance. Sat alongside the broader experimentation strategy — audience personalisation and behavioural analytics testing — with LTV as the primary success metric. Built reporting frameworks and dashboards in Amplitude and Looker to track impact and support senior stakeholder decisions.

Outcome

Sharper, LTV-informed decisioning across the onsite journey, and a paywall strategy that tied model performance directly to commercial impact.

News UK · AI evaluation

Vetting AI-powered paywall vendors

→

News UK wanted to explore AI-powered paywall tooling but had no way to evaluate different vendors' underlying models against their actual business goals.

Approach

Assessed AI paywall vendors on how their models worked and what that would mean in practice, translating technical differences between them into plain business trade-offs News UK could weigh up.

Outcome

An informed, business-goal-aligned vendor decision, rather than one based on feature lists alone.

The Independent · Revenue modelling

LTV and product-mix modelling

→

The Independent needed to know whether a registration wall was actually more valuable than advertising revenue, and what mix of products would maximise overall revenue.

Approach

Built a registered-user LTV model to quantify the long-term value of a registration wall against ad revenue, plus separate product-mix analysis to identify the blend of products that maximised overall revenue.

Outcome

Reshaped how The Independent weighed subscription growth against advertising monetisation, with a model rather than a hunch behind the decision.

In Digital · Internal AI enablement

Building AI agents to ease team workload

→

Colleagues across In Digital were spending significant time on repetitive, manual tasks with no tooling built to take the load off.

Approach

Built AI agents to automate parts of that workload, and focused on building the wider team's AI skills so the tools could actually be adopted day-to-day rather than just handed over.

Outcome

Freed up colleagues' time for higher-value work, and helped build a more AI-literate team.

Subscription publisher · RFP

Vendor selection for a subscription & paywall platform

→

A client needed to replace their subscription and paywall platform but had no structured way to compare vendors against their actual requirements.

Approach

Owned the RFP end-to-end and the client relationship throughout: defined requirements, built the roadmap and timeline, coordinated vendor demos, managed procurement, and ran a hands-on audit of the client's existing website to ground the evaluation in reality rather than vendor pitches.

Outcome

A vendor selection the client's stakeholders could stand behind, backed by a clear, evidenced decision trail rather than a gut call.

Agency clients · Platform & analytics strategy

Journey mapping, CDP integration and measurement frameworks

→

Clients had onsite customer journeys and data scattered across disconnected tools, with no coherent way to track them or connect tracking back to business goals.

Approach

Advised on platform and analytics tool selection to support onsite journeys and CDP integrations, then mapped end-to-end user journeys myself and implemented the tracking that fed each client's measurement framework.

Outcome

Journeys and tracking tied cleanly back to business and marketing goals — giving clients a measurement foundation they could actually act on, not just report against.
